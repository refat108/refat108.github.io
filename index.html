About Me

I am an Economics PhD student at the University of New Mexico. My academic and professional interests include health economics, policy analysis, econometric modeling, data management, and applied research.

Before beginning my PhD, I worked for the Central Bank of Bangladesh, where I gained experience in financial regulation, legal analysis, banking operations, and risk management. I also have experience working with survey data, cleaning and transforming datasets, and conducting benefit-cost analysis.
